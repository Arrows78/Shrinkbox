Shrinkbox 0.2.0 redesigns the floating results and the drop zone, and adds most of what was missing around them.

### Highlights

- **A new look**: result cards show the saving in a coloured capsule with a gauge, a ring in the colour of the kind of file and round buttons that light up; a conversion that makes a file bigger now says so. The drop zone is a dark glass panel with a marching border and its preset zones as labelled tiles.
- **Compare before and after** with a draggable divider (images), synchronised players (videos), an A/B switch (audio) or two pages (PDFs).
- **Crop and resize window** with a live preview, a locked aspect ratio, named sizes you keep, and paper and device sizes to start from.
- **Ready-made pipelines** (WebP, half size, video to GIF, 2× silent, pages as images, MP3…) in the menu of a result and as buttons; AirDrop, shelf apps (Yoink, Dropover, Dockside, Atoll) and Dropshare.
- **Keyboard**: act on the result under the pointer with the trigger keys, skip the next clipboard optimisation, stop everything, optimise the clipboard aggressively, and see short on-screen messages.
- **Batch review**: adjust compression, downscale, crop, conversion, speed and bitrate before a batch starts, and remove files from it.
- **Photos**: a photo copied in Photos is replaced by its original; a copied link or path is optimised too.
- **Settings**: build pipelines from a step menu, edit them, add automations and preset zones, install the command in one click, three menu bar icons.
- **Better media tools**: gifski and exiftool are now really used when installed, watched videos have resolution filters, and playback speed and cover art can be changed from a result.

### Fixed

- `shrinkbox` run through the Homebrew link used other settings than the app and printed no version.

### Good to know

- Secure peer-to-peer sending, iCloud settings sync and Shortcuts actions are not part of Shrinkbox: they need a signalling service, a Developer ID or an Xcode build.
- The Spanish and German texts are machine translations that no native speaker has reviewed.
